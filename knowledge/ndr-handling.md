# NDR handling by failure reason

## Customer unavailable
When the courier reports the customer was not home or did not answer, reschedule the delivery within the next two days. Choose the customer's preferred delivery window when a slot exists in it. Keep the same courier unless the courier has no capacity. Tell the customer the new date and window.

## Address issue
When the courier reports a wrong, incomplete or unreachable address, do not reattempt at the same address. Contact the customer to confirm the address first. If the customer cannot be reached within one day, escalate to operations. Never guess a corrected address.

## Customer refused
When the customer refuses the parcel, do not reschedule. Escalate to operations so they can confirm the refusal and start a return to origin (RTO). Record the refusal reason in the incident.

## Cash on delivery payment issue
When a cash-on-delivery (COD) customer could not pay at the door, offer a reschedule in the preferred window and remind them to keep the exact amount ready. After a second COD payment failure, escalate to operations for a prepaid payment link or RTO.

## Customer request
When the customer replies with a preferred date or time, honour the requested window if a slot exists and policy allows it. If the request cannot be met, offer the nearest slot and explain why. A customer's own request takes priority over the stored preference.
