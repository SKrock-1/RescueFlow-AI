# Office and commercial address deliveries

## Business hours only
Deliveries to offices, tech parks, towers and other commercial buildings must be scheduled between 09:00 and 18:00 on weekdays. Never schedule an evening or weekend window for a commercial address.

## Reception and security
Leave office parcels at reception or with security when the customer is not at their desk, unless the checkout note forbids it. Ask the customer for any gate pass or visitor code before the attempt.

## Call before arrival
For commercial addresses, call the customer ten to fifteen minutes before arrival so they can come to reception. Record a failed call as customer unavailable, not as an address issue.
