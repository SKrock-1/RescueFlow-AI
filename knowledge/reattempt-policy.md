# Delivery reattempt policy

## Attempt limit
A delivery may be attempted at most three times. After the third failed attempt the order must be escalated to operations instead of rescheduled again. Automatic rescheduling after three failures is not allowed.

## Choosing a slot
Pick the earliest slot that matches the customer's preferred window. If no preferred slot exists in the next two days, pick the highest-scoring available slot and say that the preference could not be met. Never schedule outside the assigned courier's working hours.

## Spacing between attempts
Leave at least four hours between a failed attempt and the next attempt on the same day. A same-day reattempt is only allowed when the customer has confirmed they are available.

## Courier changes
Keep the original courier when they have capacity, because they know the route. Reassign only to an active courier who serves the same area. Never assign an inactive or on-break courier.
