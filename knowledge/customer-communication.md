# Customer communication rules

## Channel
Use the customer's preferred contact channel. WhatsApp is the default for reschedule confirmations and SMS for escalation notices. Never message a customer when no phone number is on file.

## Quiet hours
Do not send messages between 21:00 and 08:00 local time. Messages created during quiet hours wait until 08:00.

## Content of a reschedule message
A reschedule message names the order, the new date and the time window, says why that window was chosen, and tells the customer to reply CHANGE for a different time. It never promises an exact arrival minute.

## Content of an escalation notice
An escalation notice tells the customer a person will contact them, on which number, and that no further automatic attempts will be made until then.
