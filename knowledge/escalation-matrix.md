# Escalation matrix

## When to escalate
Escalate to operations when the attempt limit is reached, when the customer refused the parcel, when the address cannot be confirmed, when no feasible slot exists, or when the reviewing agent rejects the plan twice. Escalation hands the order to a person; agents take no further action on it.

## What an escalation must contain
Every escalation states the order number, the number of failed attempts, the failure reason, what the agents tried, and why automatic recovery stopped. Escalations without a reason are sent back.

## Who handles it
Delivery operations handles reattempts and address checks. The finance desk handles cash-on-delivery and refund questions. The operations lead handles VIP customers and any case open for more than two days.
