# VIP and repeat customers

## Who counts as VIP
A customer is VIP when the store tags them as a VIP or repeat buyer, or their lifetime spend is high. VIP status comes from the customer profile and stored memory, never from the agent's guess.

## Priority handling
For VIP customers, choose the earliest slot in their preferred window and prefer a van or top-rated courier for fragile or high-value parcels. Avoid delays of more than one day. Send the reschedule message as soon as the new slot is confirmed.

## Early escalation for VIPs
After a second failed attempt for a VIP customer, notify the operations lead even if a reschedule is still allowed, so a person can call the customer personally.
